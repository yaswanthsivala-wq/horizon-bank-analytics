## Business Context & Strategic Objectives

Horizon Community Bank is a fictional regional financial institution addressing critical operational reporting challenges:
- **Siloed Systems:** Separate reporting pipelines across Core Banking, Loan Servicing, Fraud Monitoring, and CRM.
- **Reporting Cycle Bottlenecks:** Month-end reporting required three full business days of manual effort; target is **under four hours**.
- **Data Latency:** Target validated, reconciled daily metrics by **6:00 a.m. Central Time**.
- **Customer Risk Visibility:** Fragmented risk indicators across departments; target unified, explainable classification without unverified black-box scoring.

Delivery operates strictly under a synthetic data model: 24 months of synthetic history, daily batches, Python/PostgreSQL/Power BI stack, and zero paid enterprise infrastructure. The [project charter](project-charter.md) defines the full baseline scope and exclusions.
